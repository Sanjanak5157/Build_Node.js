The interview process at Webknot Technologies typically consists of multiple rounds evaluating core computer science fundamentals, coding/problem-solving, low-level design, and project-based experience.

Selection Process Rounds

• Round 1 (Written / Online Assessment): Usually includes MCQs on development stacks and computer science fundamentals, alongside coding problems (such as Longest Increasing Subsequence, Balanced Binary Tree, Matrix DP, and Grouping Anagrams).
• Round 2 (Coding Round): Focused on algorithmic problem-solving and dynamic programming (e.g., Coin Exchange problem).
• Round 3 (Technical Round 1): In-depth discussion of resume projects, tech stacks used, role contribution, and matrix/array-based Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA).
• Round 4 (Technical Round 2): Low-Level Design (LLD) questions, such as designing classes and methods for an e-commerce cart (e.g., Amazon Cart).
• Round 5 (HR Round): Behavioral questions, cultural fit, communication skills, and understanding of the company.
